# Final visual system

- Deep classic royal-blue game surfaces
- Glossy red primary actions and title accents
- Restrained gold premium accents
- Warm ivory score paper with blue rule lines
- App icon: Schmiddi & Schreier Spezial artwork integrated for PWA/iOS
- Dice: ivory-white physical material, rounded/beveled corners, recessed glossy black pips, soft specular highlights, contact shadows, subtle per-die resting rotation
- Rolling animation: staggered tumble/bounce while preserving held dice

Gameplay/scoring and V5 multiplayer round-advance logic are unchanged.
